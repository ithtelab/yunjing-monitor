# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or point of view.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Give context, reproduction steps, and evidence when reporting problems.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's credentials or private infrastructure data.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and release timing.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Spam, fraud, illegal content, or attempts to misuse the project against systems without authorization.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions, issue tracking, or project infrastructure.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Serious security or safety concerns should be reported privately through GitHub Security Advisories.
